# Zeph Rides — 30-Day Roadmap

## Mission

Move the account from an early repetitive POV feed into a deliberate motorcycle creator brand with professional visual range, stronger hooks, recurring series, disciplined analytics, and a production pipeline that can scale.

## Phase 0 — Immediate correction (next 3 posts)

Do not publish three more near-identical road covers.

Required actions:
- choose materially different cover families for the next visible row;
- include at least one rider/bike or detail-led visual;
- include one post whose concept is not simply scenic riding;
- score every post with `QA_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`;
- log cover type in `content/cover_plan.csv`;
- run `python scripts/lint_content_plan.py` before scheduling a new batch.

## Phase 1 — Rebuild the visible grid (days 1–7)

Goal: make the first 9 visible posts look like a creator feed rather than repeated ride exports.

Target mix:
- 2 road/curve POV
- 2 rider/bike hero
- 1 encounter/story
- 1 humor concept
- 1 machine/detail
- 1 scenery/mood
- 1 experiment/graphic/other

Priority production:
1. locate and cut the turkey encounter;
2. locate and cut the older gentleman thumbs-up encounter;
3. create one strong helmet-on rider/bike hero clip;
4. create one clean bike-detail/startup clip;
5. produce one 7–9 second seamless loop;
6. produce one structured 60–75 second original;
7. redesign all planned covers before they enter the queue.

## Phase 2 — Establish recurring formats (days 8–14)

Run at least one execution each of:
- WV Road Test
- Ride Encounters
- One More Corner
- Helmet On
- Machine Details
- 60 Seconds of WV

Use `analytics/experiments.csv` to document what changed and why.

At the end of day 14:
- identify top 3 hooks;
- identify top 2 cover families;
- identify top 2 content series;
- identify the weakest repeating pattern;
- retire at least one low-value pattern if evidence supports it.

## Phase 3 — Conversion optimization (days 15–21)

Focus not just on views, but on whether viewers become profile visitors and followers.

Actions:
- ensure profile's visible grid has strong variation;
- identify 3 strongest posts for potential pinning in TikTok;
- create one `what this account is` identity post without making it a corporate introduction;
- test rider presence versus pure POV;
- test recurring-series labels;
- reply to comments with personality and identify legitimate reply-video opportunities.

## Phase 4 — Scale what works (days 22–30)

By this point, increase production only for formats with evidence.

For each winning format:
- define the invariant: what makes the format itself work;
- define the variables: hook, location, encounter, joke, visual, audio;
- create 3 new executions that share the format but are not duplicates;
- keep cover composition materially different.

Do not scale a post solely because it got the most views if follower conversion and retention were poor.

## Weekly production target

Target output only when quality allows:
- 4–6 high-quality shorts
- 1–2 60–75 second originals
- 2+ rider/bike/detail assets to prevent POV dominance
- 1+ encounter/story if source footage supports it
- 1 deliberately experimental post

## Weekly review deliverables

Every 7 days:
- update `analytics/metrics.csv`;
- update `analytics/weekly-review.md`;
- review `analytics/experiments.csv`;
- update `content/cover_plan.csv`;
- run quality linter;
- document what to make more of;
- document what to stop.

## Professional-content milestone

The account reaches the first professional-content milestone when:
- no 9-post window looks visually repetitive;
- every post has a deliberate cover decision;
- at least 4 recurring formats have been tested;
- hooks, cover families, and formats are logged;
- posting decisions are based on measured outcomes rather than intuition alone;
- weak filler is routinely withheld instead of published;
- the feed has a recognizable rider/bike identity beyond generic road POV.

## Monetization milestone

Follower and program eligibility targets will be treated as outcomes of audience growth and original content quality, not as a reason to pad posts or chase low-quality volume. Platform requirements should be re-verified before any monetization-specific production sprint.
