# Zeph Rides creator operations

## Source of truth

Read `README.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `content/production_board.md`, `content/rapid_growth_production_order.md`, `PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md`, `QA_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`, `BRAND_BIBLE.md`, `BRAND_VOICE.md`, and the rights/source inventory for the specific post. Verify live Metricool/TikTok publishing and metrics before changing a queue entry from planned to scheduled, published, or measured. A CSV plan is not a published video.

## Continue protocol

When asked to continue, reconcile the visible account, scheduled queue, source inventory, and open issues. Select the top unblocked item in the short production order. Finish a real master, cover, caption, source/rights record, QA score, and posting handoff before opening another concept. Update `content/production_board.md` and the short order at the end of the session. Observe its WIP cap of three shorts and one longer original.

Use states **idea -> source confirmed -> edited master -> QA passed -> scheduled -> published -> measured**. State the exact asset location, post URL or scheduler ID, and timestamp when moving a state. Never call a brief, static preview, or queued CSV row a finished or published video.

## Creative and release gates

- Favor original, recognizable Zeph Rides humor and animated rider scenarios, including follow-ups to the high-beam format. Vary the premise and payoff; do not recycle the same clip, cover, or joke with new text.
- Keep the rider's face covered and exclude home, garage, and identifying residential footage. Do not imply dangerous riding as a challenge; capture stationary talk/LIVE material safely.
- Track provenance and usable rights for footage, memes, music, images, voice, and sponsor claims. Original commentary must add real value to any permitted remix. Hold ambiguous rights or privacy cases.
- Apply `QA_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`: default threshold 18/22, with no zero in Hook, Visual, Audio, Cover, or Truth/Rights. Check the actual 9:16 export, first second, caption safe area, audio intelligibility, thumbnail variation, and playback on a phone.
- Schedule only an existing QA-passed master with the correct caption and cover. Do not buy a tool upgrade merely to fill slots; use the documented native handoff when a scheduler limit is reached.
- Review 2-hour, 24-hour, and 72-hour results when available. Distinguish organic from promoted reach and prioritize retention, shares, profile visits, and followers over raw views alone. Recheck platform eligibility before any monetization claim.

## Report format

End each session with **published/scheduled evidence**, **finished assets awaiting handoff**, **measured learning**, **blocked source or account step**, and **the next one to three actions**. Report missing platform data as missing, never estimated as fact.
