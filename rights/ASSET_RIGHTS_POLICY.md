# Asset Rights & Sourcing Policy

## Purpose

Zeph Rides may use original footage, original stills, generated graphics, and properly licensed external assets. This policy exists so visual quality can increase without creating avoidable copyright, licensing, or trust problems.

## Allowed asset classes

1. **Original** — footage or stills captured by us.
2. **Commissioned / created for Zeph Rides** — graphics, edits, or images made specifically for the brand with rights sufficient for publication.
3. **Generated** — AI-generated material created for the channel, provided it is not misleading and passes realism/quality review.
4. **Public domain / CC0** — verified source and status recorded.
5. **Licensed stock / external** — license permits the intended social/commercial use.
6. **Platform-provided audio/creative assets** — used according to the platform's current terms and monetization rules.

## Not allowed

- random Google Images results;
- screenshots or images copied from other creators without permission;
- watermarked stock previews;
- assets with unclear commercial-use rights when monetization is intended;
- misleading generated images presented as real ride events;
- logos/brands used in a way that falsely implies sponsorship;
- another rider's footage reposted without documented permission or a valid platform-native remix context.

## Source record

For every non-original visual asset that reaches publication, record:
- asset filename/identifier;
- source URL or provider;
- creator/provider name if applicable;
- license/permission basis;
- date acquired;
- permitted use notes;
- whether attribution is required;
- where it was used.

## Generated media

Generated assets should be labeled internally as GENERATED even when platform disclosure is not required.

Do not use generated media to fabricate:
- crashes;
- police interactions;
- dangerous incidents;
- public reactions;
- wildlife encounters;
- specific road events;
- product behavior;
- anything else viewers would reasonably interpret as documentary footage from the ride.

Generated media is appropriate for:
- stylized thumbnail art;
- abstract graphic concepts;
- non-documentary title cards;
- mood/brand creative;
- visual ideation.

## Cover-specific rule

A cover may be separately created, but it must truthfully represent the subject of the post. If the post is a simple road clip, do not use a dramatic animal, crash, police car, or stranger on the cover unless that subject actually appears in the post.

## Music/audio

For monetization-focused originals, prefer original audio, properly licensed music, or audio explicitly permitted for the relevant use. Platform availability does not automatically mean every sound is eligible for every monetization program.

## Review gate

If rights are uncertain, HOLD the post until they are resolved. Visual variety is not worth a copyright problem.
