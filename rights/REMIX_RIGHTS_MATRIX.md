# Remix Rights / Platform Reuse Matrix

This is an operational guide, not legal advice. When rights are uncertain, HOLD.

| Source situation | Default action | Publish as our own post? | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| TikTok native Repost | Repost natively | No new owned video | Safest community-share lane; do not download/re-upload. |
| TikTok Stitch enabled | Create natively in Stitch | Yes, as Stitch | Source creator remains linked/attributed by TikTok; our contribution still needs substantive value. |
| TikTok Duet enabled | Create natively in Duet | Yes, as Duet | Best when side-by-side playback is integral to the concept. |
| Creator explicitly grants permission | Keep proof of permission, scope edit carefully | Yes | Log creator, URL, date, permission terms, attribution expectations. |
| Licensed stock/public-domain footage | Verify license and intended commercial/social use | Yes | Preserve source/license evidence. |
| Video downloadable but no Stitch/Duet/permission | Do not re-upload | No | Technical access is not permission. |
| Screenshot/meme copied from another creator | Do not use by default | No | Recreate the joke with our own imagery or obtain permission. |
| Platform-native sound/trend | Use according to current platform rules | Usually | Monetization eligibility can differ from ordinary posting rights. |
| News/event clip | Case-by-case review | HOLD by default | Credit alone does not create permission or guarantee fair use. |
| Crash / injury clip from another rider | Avoid as growth bait | HOLD | Higher privacy, dignity, safety and rights risk; little brand upside. |

## Native remix rule

When the content is usable through TikTok's own Stitch or Duet control, prefer that native path. Do not extract the source clip and rebuild a fake Stitch in an external editor merely to remove attribution or gain more control.

## Attribution

Native attribution is the floor, not a reason to obscure the source creator. When useful to viewers, mention the creator naturally in the caption or on-screen response.

## Transformative-value gate

For Zeph Rides, a community remix is not approved merely because a platform feature allows it. Our addition should contribute at least one of:
- substantive commentary;
- a new joke/punchline;
- a personal riding story;
- comparison with our own footage/bike;
- useful context;
- a distinct reaction that is actually the entertainment;
- a new question or community discussion.

## Monetization handling

Keep remix/community posts analytically distinct from original one-minute productions. Do not assume native reuse makes a video eligible for Creator Rewards. Original monetization-track videos should remain clearly original Zeph Rides works unless current program rules explicitly support the format.

## Evidence to log

For any non-original source used beyond a simple native Repost, record in `content/remix_source_queue.csv` or the production brief:
- source URL;
- creator;
- reuse mode;
- permission/license basis;
- date checked;
- our added-value concept;
- final post URL;
- any attribution provided.

## Remix decision rule — added 2026-09-21

The default community action is native Stitch, Duet, Repost, or a permission-cleared response. Download-and-reupload is not an acceptable default workflow.

Before any community asset enters production, record:

- source URL and creator;
- native reuse option;
- rights or permission status;
- the exact new joke, explanation, comparison, or response Zeph adds;
- planned platform and attribution;
- whether the asset remains eligible for the intended monetization lane.

If the new value cannot be explained in one sentence, skip the source and build an original graphic or riding edit instead. See rights/REMIX_WORKFLOW.md.
