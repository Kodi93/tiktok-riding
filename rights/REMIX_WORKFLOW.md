# Zeph Rides — Remix Workflow and Rights Guardrails

This workflow exists to use community content for discovery without turning the account into a repost page.

## Priority order

1. Native TikTok Stitch or Duet with a real Zeph Rides response.
2. Native TikTok Repost when the goal is conversation, not ownership.
3. Direct permission from the creator, recorded in the source queue.
4. Public-domain or clearly licensed media with the license/source recorded.
5. Original recreation inspired by a format, without copying the source video.

The default deliverable should be an inspired original or a native commentary format, not a downloaded copy.

## Do not use

- downloader sites or ripped files as the normal sourcing method;
- another creator's full clip with a new caption;
- cropped or blurred watermarks;
- music ripped from another platform;
- content whose source, creator, or reuse status cannot be identified;
- private, deleted, paywalled, or leaked content;
- staged danger, humiliation, harassment, or identifiable bystander footage without a legitimate reason and permission.

## What makes a remix materially new

A remix should add a clear new layer such as:

- a joke with a different punchline;
- a rider-specific explanation;
- a comparison with the Z7 Hybrid;
- a safety or mechanical observation;
- a visual reaction that changes the meaning;
- an answer to a question;
- a short editorial argument that the source alone does not contain.

A caption alone is not enough. There is no safe percentage rule that turns copying into fair use. Use the smallest source portion necessary and prefer the platform's native reuse tools.

## Source queue requirements

Every candidate needs:

- source URL;
- creator/account;
- discovery date;
- native reuse option visible or not visible;
- permission status;
- proposed new angle;
- planned platform;
- whether the post is original, Stitch, Duet, Repost, or permission-cleared;
- attribution language where appropriate;
- removal/hold reason if rights are unclear.

## Platform split

For TikTok:

- prioritize native Stitch, Duet, and reply-video tools;
- keep the Zeph response visually dominant;
- retain the original creator attribution supplied by the platform;
- do not promise monetization value from community material.

For Facebook:

- use native share when the goal is to point people to the original;
- otherwise use owned graphics or permission-cleared material;
- never use a TikTok watermark;
- keep the Facebook brand separate from the personal TikTok rider identity.

## Approval rule

If the editor cannot explain in one sentence what new value Zeph adds, the candidate is SKIP.

If the source is not clearly reusable, do not download it. Recreate the joke with owned footage or graphic animation instead.
