# Zeph Rides — Brand Voice

## Voice in one sentence
A rider who notices the funny, weird, satisfying, and slightly irrational parts of motorcycle life — confident, concise, human, and never trying too hard.

## Core traits
- playful without becoming corny;
- self-aware without self-deprecation becoming the whole brand;
- rider-native rather than corporate;
- curious about roads, machines, and people;
- Appalachian/WV character without forcing regional stereotypes;
- visually mysterious through the helmet-on identity;
- opinionated enough to be interesting without manufacturing outrage.

## What Zeph Rides sounds like
Good:
- `Quick ride. Famous last words.`
- `Technically, I am still on my way home.`
- `That back road looked like a terrible idea. So obviously...`
- `The GPS had a plan. I had a different one.`
- `One more corner is not a unit of time.`
- `I was absolutely going home too.`

Bad:
- generic motivational quotes;
- fake tough-guy biker language;
- constant `POV:` openings;
- engagement bait like `only real riders understand`;
- fake controversy;
- overexplaining every joke;
- obvious AI filler such as `there is just something magical about the open road`.

## Caption rule
Captions should add one of four things:
1. a second joke;
2. a useful bit of context;
3. a natural question worth answering;
4. a search-friendly phrase that still sounds human.

Do not repeat the exact on-screen text in the caption unless needed for accessibility/context.

## Comment voice
- answer real questions directly;
- play along with good jokes;
- reward thoughtful comments;
- do not over-defend the bike/account;
- do not argue with obvious trolls for reach;
- use disagreement as a potential reply-video prompt only when there is a real point to make.

## Meme language
Reaction text should be short and instantly readable. Preferred energy:
- `SELF CONTROL: 0%`
- `BAD IDEA APPROVED`
- `BIKE BRAIN ACTIVATED`
- `ROAD WON`
- `ETA: EVENTUALLY`
- `WIFE WAS RIGHT`
- `CORE MEMORY UNLOCKED`
- `WV ROUTE PLANNER`

Use these as a pattern language, not identical repeated stamps.

## TTS language
TTS should sound like a person wrote it for the clip, not a narrator describing stock footage.

Prefer:
- `I said I was heading home. Then this road showed up.`
- `This was supposed to be a twenty-minute ride.`
- `I knew exactly where I was going. That was the first mistake.`

Avoid:
- `Join me on an unforgettable motorcycle adventure through beautiful West Virginia.`
- `Today we embark on an exciting journey.`
- `Motorcycles are more than machines; they are freedom.`

## Humor boundaries
The account can be chaotic, sarcastic, and occasionally absurd. It should not become mean-spirited, humiliating, or dependent on dangerous riding.

The rider is often the butt of the joke: bad route discipline, `one more road`, gear expense, gas stops, bugs, weather, and motorcycle-owner logic.

## Hot takes
Good hot takes are specific and defensible:
- hybrid/automatic motorcycle assumptions;
- whether a road is actually worth the detour;
- gear convenience;
- everyday rider experience.

Avoid outrage bait whose only purpose is comments.

## Anti-repetition
Across six consecutive posts:
- vary sentence structure;
- do not use the same punchline ending more than once;
- avoid repeating `the road had other plans` unless deliberately testing it;
- do not turn every caption into a question;
- do not use the same meme card repeatedly.

## Final test
Before publishing copy, ask: `Would a real rider text this to another rider?`

If not, rewrite it.
