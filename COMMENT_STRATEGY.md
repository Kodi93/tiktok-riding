# Zeph Rides — Comment & Reply-Video Strategy

## Purpose
Comments are not just engagement to answer. They are a live source of objections, jokes, questions, stories, and future video prompts.

## Response priority
Respond first to comments that can deepen identity or create a new content opportunity:
- genuine questions about the bike;
- strong reactions to unusual road/encounter moments;
- good jokes we can build on;
- requests for more detail;
- reasonable disagreement;
- comments that expose a recurring misconception;
- prompts that naturally lead to another ride clip.

Low priority:
- generic emoji-only comments;
- obvious bait;
- repetitive insults;
- arguments with no useful content angle.

## Reply types
### 1. Direct answer
Use when the viewer asks for a simple factual answer. Keep it concise and verify technical claims.

### 2. Play-along reply
Use when the viewer makes a good joke. Match the energy without forcing brand slogans.

### 3. Follow-up question
Use when the comment opens a real conversation. Do not ask empty questions only to farm replies.

### 4. Reply video
Escalate to a video when:
- the answer is visually demonstrable;
- the question is likely shared by many viewers;
- we have strong footage that answers it;
- the comment creates a clean hook;
- a hot take deserves evidence rather than a text argument.

## Reply-video formula
1. show/quote the comment immediately;
2. answer within the first 1–2 seconds;
3. use footage as evidence;
4. keep the response focused on one point;
5. end with a payoff or natural next question.

Examples:
- `Is that really a hybrid?` -> startup/controls/ride behavior.
- `Why would you turn down that road?` -> show the hard-left decision and payoff.
- `WV roads can't be that twisty.` -> visual proof.
- `Automatic isn't a real motorcycle.` -> concise owner POV + actual riding clip.

## Comment-to-content queue
Every promising comment should be logged with:
- source post;
- commenter text/summary;
- response type;
- footage needed;
- series fit;
- urgency/shelf life;
- expected value: reach, comments, follows, search, or identity.

## Tone
Use `BRAND_VOICE.md`.

Never let one troll drag the account into an argument chain. If disagreement can become useful content, turn it into a clean reply video; otherwise move on.

## Moderation principles
Hide/report only where appropriate under platform rules; do not manufacture conflict for reach. Do not expose private information, encourage harassment, or dogpile other creators.

## Success metrics
Track whether reply videos produce:
- above-baseline completion;
- comments per 1,000 views;
- profile visits;
- follows;
- repeat questions that justify a series.

The lane is working when comments begin generating the next strong posts instead of being treated as a dead-end after publishing.
