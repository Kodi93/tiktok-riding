# Professional Batch Review

Use this before scheduling each new content batch.

## Grid review
- Does the 3-column profile preview show obvious visual variety?
- Are any three adjacent covers substantially the same?
- Does the batch include enough rider/bike/personality imagery to avoid looking like anonymous dashcam footage?
- Are there weak covers that need a dedicated still, generated graphic, or legally usable sourced asset?

## Creative review
For each post, confirm:
- first-second hook
- visual focal point
- story/payoff
- edit pace
- audio treatment
- on-screen text
- caption
- thumbnail/cover concept
- differentiation from adjacent posts
- expected content pillar
- intended KPI to test

## Reject / revise conditions
Revise rather than schedule when:
- the opening looks generic
- the edit feels like filler
- the cover duplicates nearby posts
- the text sounds templated or AI-generic
- the footage has no clear reason to watch through
- the audio is weak or distracting
- a stronger version can realistically be produced

## Approval
A batch should be treated as publish-ready only when it meets `PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md` and `content/thumbnail_strategy.md`.
