# Pinned Post Strategy

Do not pin posts merely because they have the highest view count. Pins should explain the account and convert profile visitors into followers.

## Ideal three-pin stack

### Pin 1 — Identity
A strong rider/bike piece that immediately communicates Zeph Rides visually.

### Pin 2 — Proof / standout moment
Best authentic encounter, funniest real moment, or strongest-performing representative post.

### Pin 3 — Series / depth
A structured original or recurring-series post that shows there is more to follow than isolated clips.

## Candidate criteria

A pin candidate should score well on:
- visual quality;
- immediate comprehension;
- brand identity;
- follower conversion/profile behavior where data exists;
- comments/shares;
- evergreen value;
- relevance to what the account will keep publishing.

## Avoid pinning

- a random outlier that does not represent future content;
- a repetitive road clip solely because it has slightly more views;
- an old low-quality edit that conflicts with the current standard;
- clickbait or controversial content that attracts the wrong audience;
- three posts with nearly identical covers.

## Review cadence

Re-evaluate pins after each meaningful creative shift or when a clearly stronger representative post emerges.
