# Capture Shot List

Use this list during future rides and planned shoots so the editor is not trapped with nothing but forward-facing road footage.

## Rule

Every ride should intentionally capture a **coverage package** in addition to POV footage. Ten minutes spent gathering inserts can create dramatically more professional edits and thumbnails.

## Before ride

Capture 5–10 seconds each:
- bike parked wide shot
- front three-quarter bike hero
- rear three-quarter bike hero
- helmet on bike / rider holding helmet only if it fits the mystery concept
- gloved hand on bar
- ignition/startup
- instrument cluster power-up
- mirror detail
- light/headlight detail
- wheel/brake/fairing close-up
- rider walking toward bike
- rider mounting bike
- kickstand up
- glove adjustment
- helmet strap/visor interaction

## During ride

Where safe and naturally captured:
- distinctive road sign
- bridge/tunnel
- sharp curve geometry
- changing weather
- unusual vehicle
- another rider interaction
- animal/wildlife encounter
- scenic reveal
- town entry/exit
- fuel/charge/stop moment
- mirror reaction/context

Do not stage or chase risky road events for content.

## At stops

Capture:
- motorcycle against scenery
- vertical portrait of rider + bike
- silhouette
- close bike detail with environment blurred/backgrounded
- road disappearing into distance
- sign/location marker
- helmet reflection
- gloves on seat/tank only if visually clean and safe for surfaces
- boots/bike lower-angle composition
- 3–5 second static clips for edit breathing room
- one clean photo specifically intended as a future cover

## End of ride

Capture:
- arrival/parking
- ignition off
- instrument cluster shutdown
- glove removal
- bike cooling/ambient sound
- dusk/night lighting if available
- one final rider/bike silhouette

## Cover-bank target

For every meaningful ride, aim to leave with at least:
- 2 rider/bike cover candidates
- 2 bike-detail cover candidates
- 2 environment/road cover candidates
- 1 unusual/encounter candidate if a real moment occurs
- 1 night/mood candidate when conditions permit

## Technical capture notes

- Prefer vertical capture for TikTok-first assets.
- Keep the subject large enough to survive profile-grid cropping.
- Hold static compositions for several seconds; do not whip the camera away immediately.
- Check lens cleanliness before hero/detail shots.
- Capture some clips without text baked in so they remain reusable.
- Keep original files; avoid repeated re-encoding before final export.

## Safety

Never create a shot list requirement that encourages unsafe operation of the motorcycle, distracted riding, dangerous roadside positioning, trespass, or interference with traffic. Professional content is not worth increasing riding risk.
