# Facebook Queue — Clutch Tribunal

This file lives in the TikTok riding repository for operational convenience only.

**Brand separation rule:** Clutch Tribunal is the Facebook-only motorcycle entertainment brand. Do not reference Zeph Rides, `@zeph.rides.wv`, or reuse Zeph-specific branding in these posts. Keep the two identities operationally separate even though they share this repository.

## Queue rules

- Statuses: IDEA, READY, SCHEDULED, PUBLISHED, REVIEW, RETIRED.
- Avoid exact duplicate concepts/copy within seven days.
- Every feed post needs a strong first line and a comment-driving close.
- Pair text posts with original or permission-cleared motorcycle/helmet/road imagery when useful.
- Check performance at roughly 2h / 24h / 72h and record what actually drove comments/shares.
- Tone target: highly relatable rider humor, slightly chaotic, mildly unhinged, easy to skim.

`relatable rider problem -> escalating short lines -> exaggerated punchline -> direct rider question`

Keep copy native to Facebook: conversational, easy to skim, and comment-oriented. Do not force a link, hashtag stack, or TikTok-style caption onto these posts.
