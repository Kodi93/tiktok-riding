## Backlog acceptance rule

A concept should not enter production unless we can answer:
1. What is the viewer getting from this?
2. What is visually different from recent posts?
3. What happens in the first second?
4. What is the payoff?
5. What cover family will it use?
6. Which series/brand pillar does it strengthen?
7. What metric would tell us whether to repeat the format?

## Production rule

Do not render the entire backlog at once. Produce a deliberately varied batch, publish, measure, then select the next batch using evidence.

**Never produce filler simply to maintain two posts per day.** A held slot is preferable to another repetitive, low-value road upload.
