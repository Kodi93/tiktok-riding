# Thumbnail / Cover Strategy

## Non-negotiable rule

The TikTok profile grid must have **significant visual variation between adjacent posts**. We are not going to let the account become a wall of nearly identical road POV frames, even when the underlying videos come from the same ride.

Thumbnail/cover diversity is a production requirement, not an afterthought.

## Why

Early posts are clustering around similar road, pavement, tree-line, and guardrail imagery. Even when the videos differ, the profile grid can make them look like the same post repeated. That weakens curiosity, makes the account feel less varied, and gives returning viewers fewer visual reasons to tap.

## Required variation

For every production batch:

- Do not allow more than **two adjacent covers** with the same basic composition.
- Avoid repeating the same road angle, horizon position, tree/guardrail layout, or bike-shadow silhouette in neighboring posts.
- Rotate among clearly different cover types: rider/bike, close-up detail, road/curve, unusual encounter, destination/scenery, humor/reaction, text-led graphic, night/low-light, mechanical detail, and human/lifestyle imagery.
- Change crop, scale, focal point, text placement, and visual density between posts.
- Use cover text sparingly and make the wording visually distinct when text is used.
- Prefer a cover that creates a question or tension rather than simply showing another generic road frame.

## Source hierarchy

Use the strongest available source in this order:

1. A compelling frame from the actual video.
2. A separately captured still/photo from the same ride or scene.
3. Another original photo of the rider, motorcycle, gear, destination, or relevant subject.
4. A purpose-built graphic or AI-generated visual that accurately represents the post and is not misleading.
5. A legally usable/licensed external image when it is genuinely relevant and platform rules permit it.

We may source or generate imagery specifically for covers when needed to keep the grid visually varied. **Do not use unrelated clickbait imagery or imply that something happened in the video when it did not.**

If TikTok/Metricool does not allow an independent custom thumbnail for the account/post type, create or capture a suitable in-video frame that can be selected as the cover rather than accepting a repetitive default frame.

## Batch planning gate

Before a batch is scheduled, review the planned covers together as a 3-column profile grid. If the grid reads as repetitive at a glance, the batch is not ready.

Every scheduled-post record should include:

- cover concept/type
- intended cover frame or source asset
- cover text, if any
- whether the neighboring scheduled covers are visually distinct

## Current corrective action

The existing profile currently overuses similar road-POV cover imagery. Upcoming posts should deliberately over-correct toward greater visual diversity until the grid has a healthier mix of motorcycle, rider, encounter, scenery, humor, close-up, and graphic-led covers.
