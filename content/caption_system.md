# Caption System

## Objective

Captions should strengthen the video, not duplicate the hook or read like automated engagement bait.

## Caption jobs

A caption should primarily do one of these:
- add context the video does not have time to explain;
- continue the joke;
- ask a specific opinion;
- label a recurring series;
- add a searchable/location-relevant phrase naturally;
- give a soft reason to follow the series/account.

## Caption patterns

### Context
`Took the long route home and found this stretch by accident.`

### Specific question
`Would you keep following this road or turn around here?`

### Humor continuation
`For legal purposes, “quick ride” has no defined duration.`

### Series label
`WV Road Test #01 — this one escalated quickly.`

### Identity
`Helmet stays on. Camera stays rolling.`

## Avoid

- `Thoughts?` with no context
- `Agree?`
- `Who else?`
- `Wait for it` when there is no payoff
- five unrelated hashtags
- generic inspirational paragraphs
- repeating the exact on-screen sentence
- stuffing keywords until the caption sounds unnatural

## Hashtag rule

Use a small set of genuinely relevant tags rather than a wall of generic discovery tags. Prefer category/location/series relevance over random trending tags.

Examples may include motorcycle/riding/WV-specific tags when appropriate, but hashtag performance should be treated as secondary to the creative itself.

## CTA rule

Do not put `follow for more` on every post. Use conversion prompts only when there is a real reason:
- recurring series;
- continuation;
- audience asked for follow-up;
- a clear promise of future similar value.

Better: `I have three more WV roads like this saved.`  
Worse: `FOLLOW FOR PART 2` when no part 2 exists.

## Comment seeding

The account should not fabricate comments or fake controversy. The caption can invite real discussion through a specific question tied to the video.

## Caption QA

Before posting:
- Does it add something new?
- Does it sound like a person?
- Is the question specific?
- Is it truthful?
- Is it different from the last few captions?
- Would the post still work without it? If not, the video may need stronger storytelling.
