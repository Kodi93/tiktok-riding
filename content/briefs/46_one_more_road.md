# Content Brief — 46_one_more_road

## Asset
- Working name: `46_one_more_road`
- Source footage: locate clip where rider takes a hard left from the main road onto a back road
- Series: One More Corner / Meme Reaction Insert
- Format: original Zeph short
- Target duration: 7–10 seconds
- Priority: P0

## Viewer value
Relatable rider joke: the rider says he is going home, sees one tempting back road, and immediately abandons the plan.

## Opening
- Frame-zero visual: stable forward ride on main road, already moving
- Text: `Me: alright, I'm heading home`
- First cut: before the left-turn decision becomes obvious

## Story beats
1. **Setup (0.0–1.4s):** main-road riding; `Me: alright, I'm heading home`
2. **Pressure (1.4–2.5s):** `My wife: you coming home?`
3. **Temptation (2.5–3.7s):** reveal the back road / begin left-turn setup; text: `That back road:`
4. **Reaction hit (roughly 3.7–4.5s):** original Zeph `OH YEAH`-energy chaos-approval insert, timed to commitment into the turn
5. **Payoff (4.5–7.5s):** immediately return to real footage entering the back road
6. **Optional close (7.5–9.0s):** `Yeah... I'm gonna be late.` or `ETA home: eventually.`

## Editing direction
- The actual hard-left commitment is the hero beat. Do not hide it behind the meme insert.
- Reaction insert should be approximately 0.5–0.9 seconds unless testing proves otherwise.
- Preserve enough before/after footage that viewers understand this was a deliberate route choice.
- Hard cut or impact frame is preferred over a decorative transition.
- Keep engine audio underneath where possible; use one short impact/approval sting.
- No slow lead-in.

## Meme insert
Desired emotional reference: exaggerated `OH YEAH` / irresistible temptation / chaotic approval.

Preferred execution: **original Zeph visual**, not a ripped branded-character clip. Candidates:
- bike/helmet silhouette crashes through graphic wall + `OH YEAH`;
- `GO HOME` vs `ONE MORE ROAD` decision screen, with `ONE MORE ROAD` winning violently;
- `TERRIBLE IDEA — ACCEPTED` impact stamp;
- `SELF CONTROL: 0%` warning card.

## Cover
Preferred family: route-decision / humor.

Best cover candidates:
- frame immediately before the hard left with the side road clearly visible;
- freeze frame at commitment into the turn with short text `ONE MORE ROAD`;
- custom truthful graphic using the actual turn frame.

Avoid another generic center-road POV cover if the turn can be shown clearly.

## Caption candidates
- `I was absolutely going home.`
- `One more road is not a unit of time.`
- `ETA home: eventually.`
- `The road had other plans.`

## Experiment
Hypothesis: a clearly visible decision + meme reaction insert will outperform a plain road/TTS joke because the footage itself provides setup and payoff.

Primary metrics:
- completion rate / watch ratio;
- shares per 100 views;
- comments per 100 views;
- profile visits/follows if available.

Compare against plain TTS humor shorts of similar length.

## QA requirements
- actual left-turn clip located and source logged;
- joke understandable muted;
- reaction insert does not conceal the turn;
- no copyrighted mascot/TV/movie clip unless rights basis is clear;
- cover is distinct from adjacent road covers;
- final passes `QA_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`.
