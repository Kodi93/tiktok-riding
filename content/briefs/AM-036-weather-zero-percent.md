# AM-036 — 0% Rain

## Purpose
Exploit the strongest proven creative family: fast animated rider humor with an instantly readable premise. This is adjacent to the 1,584-view weather joke, but the setup and payoff are new.

## Runtime
6–8 seconds, vertical 9:16.

## Storyboard
**0.0–0.6s**  
Frame-zero phone/weather card: **0% CHANCE OF RAIN**

**0.6–2.2s**  
Cut to rider/road silhouette. One tiny cloud icon appears directly over the rider.

**2.2–4.8s**  
Cloud follows only the rider while the rest of the frame stays clear. Rain meter jumps from 0 → 100.

**4.8–6.8s**  
Punchline fills frame: **PERSONAL CLOUD SUBSCRIPTION ACTIVE.**

## TTS
"Zero percent chance of rain. Apparently I am the one percent."

## Audio
Short notification ping at frame zero; light comic rumble when the cloud locks on; one impact hit on punchline. No copyrighted music baked into master.

## Cover
**0% RAIN, APPARENTLY**

## Caption
Weather app said zero percent. My motorcycle apparently has premium weather. 💀🏍️ #MotorcycleHumor #BikeTok #ZephRides

## Loop
End cloud animation should visually match opening weather-card transition so replay is nearly seamless.

## QA
- premise readable in <0.5s
- payoff before 7s
- weather joke clearly fictional/comedic
- no unsafe weather-riding instruction
- visually different from the prior weather post
