# AM-040 — Clutch Tribunal: Failure to Wave

## Purpose
Launch a repeatable opinion/comment series that gives riders a reason to recognize the next episode.

## Runtime
6–8 seconds, vertical 9:16.

## Series identity
Opening stamp for 0.25–0.4s max:
**CLUTCH TRIBUNAL — CASE 001**

Do not let branding delay the actual premise.

## Storyboard
**0.0–0.7s**  
Text over a simple rider-wave silhouette: **HE DIDN'T WAVE BACK.**

**0.7–2.5s**  
Freeze frame becomes evidence card:
**DEFENSE: MAY HAVE BEEN SHIFTING**

**2.5–4.5s**  
Gavel / decision meter animates.

**4.5–6.8s**  
Verdict: **NOT GUILTY. STILL JUDGED.**

## TTS
"The court accepts that he may have been shifting. The court remains disappointed."

## Cover
**FAILURE TO WAVE**

## Caption
Clutch Tribunal, Case 001. Was he shifting or are we filing an appeal? 💀🏍️ #MotorcycleHumor #BikeTok #ZephRides

## Comment strategy
Use a real viewer disagreement as the seed for Case 002/response content. Do not manufacture fake comments.

## QA
- joke must work without audio
- no real rider is identifiable
- court visual language stays consistent enough to become a series
- total runtime under 8s
