# SS-001 — High-Beam Sequel Production Brief

## Objective

Create a materially new follow-up to the strongest scenario family without copying the original execution. The joke is recognition + escalation, not road rage.

## Deliverable

- 9:16 vertical master
- 7–9 seconds
- clean master without copyrighted music baked in
- large mobile-safe captions
- no home/garage/private-property imagery
- visor-down rider identity
- safe, normal road behavior throughout

## Storyboard

### 0.0–0.7s — Hook
Visual: stylized public two-lane mountain road at dusk/night. Oncoming headlights already visible.

On-screen text:
**HE FLASHED ME AGAIN.**

No intro logo.

### 0.7–2.5s — Setup
Oncoming car gives one fast high-beam flash.

Subtle caption:
**...okay.**

Audio:
- low motorcycle engine bed
- short light-flash stinger

### 2.5–4.6s — Deadpan hold
Hold just long enough to create anticipation.
Rider remains steady in lane. No gestures, swerving, acceleration, or confrontation.

Optional TTS:
**"Buddy... those were still the lows."**

### 4.6–6.7s — Payoff
Rider toggles actual high beam.
Use a clearly stylized comic/anime power-up treatment:
- white bloom
- lime HUD pulse
- exaggerated forward light cone
- one bass hit / comic impact

The beam should read as visual comedy, not as the rider intentionally blinding the driver.

### 6.7–8.5s — End card
**FACTORY SETTINGS WERE THE WARNING.**

Small Zeph Rides mark only if it does not slow the punchline.

## Cover

Primary:
**THOSE WERE STILL THE LOWS**

Alternative test:
**HE FLASHED ME AGAIN**

Use a frame with the bike/rider silhouette plus obvious oncoming headlights. Avoid another generic centerline-only cover.

## Caption options

A:
The factory lows apparently need a disclaimer. 🏍️

B:
Every night rider knows this exact conversation.

C:
Z7 headlights: apparently a social experiment.

Do not use a generic "follow for more" CTA.

## Comment seeding prompt

Pin only if natural:
**Night riders: how often does this happen to you?**

## Visual treatment

- premium motion-comic rather than slideshow meme
- Appalachian/WV mountain-road feel
- dark road / cool night palette
- restrained Zeph lime accents
- strong contrast
- no tiny text
- no fake TikTok UI
- no reused creator art unless licensed/owned

## Safety / brand constraints

- no road rage framing
- no aggressive pursuit
- no speeding
- no swerving
- no collision
- no blinding POV aimed at the camera for more than a very brief comic flash
- no implication the rider should retaliate against drivers
- public-road or fully generated environment only

## QA gate

Release only if:
- premise is readable with sound off by 0.5s
- joke resolves by <=8.5s
- text remains inside mobile-safe margins
- payoff looks obviously stylized
- cover is visually distinct from recent road clips
- the final cut is meaningfully different from AM-001
- master passes `scripts/validate_tiktok_master.sh`

## Measurement

Capture at 2h / 24h / 72h when available:
- organic views
- promoted views separately
- likes
- comments
- shares
- profile visits
- follows
- average watch
- completion
- rewatches

Primary signal: share rate versus the current ~0.03% account snapshot.
