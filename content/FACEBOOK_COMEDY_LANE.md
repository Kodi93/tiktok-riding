# Zeph Rides — Separate Facebook Comedy Lane

Status: active support lane, 2026-09-21

Facebook is a separate brand surface. It is not a mirror of TikTok and it must not identify or correlate the personal TikTok rider account.

## Hard separation

- Do not cross-post personal TikTok riding clips into the separate Facebook brand.
- Do not reuse TikTok handles, profile screenshots, unpublished analytics, personal location clues, or identity-revealing captions.
- Do not leave a TikTok watermark on a Facebook asset.
- Do not imply that the Facebook page and the anonymous rider account are the same person or operation.
- Use general motorcycle-culture humor, graphic cards, public-domain/licensed media, or brand-owned animation.
- Do not download and reupload other creators' videos. Use native sharing or permission-cleared material and add real editorial value.

## What Facebook should publish

Facebook rewards a slightly slower, more conversational format than the first-second TikTok test lane.

### Static meme cards

Use the Clutch Tribunal visual system:

- black or deep charcoal background;
- white headline;
- lime verdict or accent;
- one question or ruling;
- no personal address, home, or identifiable rider information.

Examples:

- “FULL GEAR FOR A FIVE-MINUTE RIDE? SENSIBLE. PARANOID. OR BOTH?”
- “THEY DIDN'T WAVE BACK. SENTENCE: OVERTHINK IT FOR THREE BUSINESS DAYS.”
- “AUTOMATIC BIKES ARE REAL BIKES. APPEAL BELOW.”
- “ALL-BLACK GEAR AT NIGHT. DRIP OR ACTIVE CAMOUFLAGE?”

### Facebook Reels

Use only clean, owned exports:

- graphic-only animated memes;
- general motorcycle humor;
- public-road footage that does not identify the personal TikTok account;
- original voiceover or platform-safe audio;
- no TikTok watermark.

### Conversation posts

Ask a question that can be answered from experience:

- “What is the shortest ride that still gets full gear?”
- “Do scooters get the wave?”
- “What motorcycle opinion would survive Clutch Tribunal?”
- “What road feature instantly changes your route?”

Avoid “only real riders understand” bait and do not pick fights with commenters.

## Weekly working rhythm

- Monday: one Clutch Tribunal verdict card.
- Wednesday: one general biker question with a strong graphic.
- Friday: one clean animated Facebook Reel or graphic sequence.
- Weekend: one comment-led poll or follow-up verdict.

Do not force the cadence when the asset is weak. Facebook should remain a clean, separate brand rather than a dumping ground for unused TikTok material.

## Facebook export checklist

- [ ] No TikTok watermark or account handle.
- [ ] No house, garage, driveway, or recognizable home setting.
- [ ] No face reveal or personal identity clue.
- [ ] Text readable without platform UI.
- [ ] Audio is cleared for Facebook or omitted.
- [ ] Source/reuse status logged.
- [ ] Caption invites an actual motorcycle-culture response.
