# Native TikTok Text-to-Speech Scripts

These scripts are written for TikTok's native Text-to-speech tool. Use the default/narrator voice first. The goal is recognizable TikTok language without making the account sound like a corporate ad.

## Timing rules

- 8–12 second video: 8–18 spoken words.
- 60–75 second video: short narration blocks separated by real visual beats.
- Use periods and ellipses for pauses.
- Put each punchline in its own text block.
- Avoid explaining the joke twice.
- Apply the voice inside TikTok before scheduling through Metricool.

## Short scripts

### 14_quick_ride_again

Text block 1: “I said I would be home by six.”

Text block 2: “That was optimistic.”

CTA: “Who always turns a quick ride into an all-day ride?”

### 15_gps_left

Text block 1: “GPS said turn left.”

Text block 2: “The road said absolutely not.”

CTA: “Do you trust the GPS or the road?”

### 16_calm_ride

Text block 1: “Today was supposed to be relaxing.”

Text block 2: “Then I saw this road.”

CTA: “Would you take the turn?”

### 17_last_corner

Text block 1: “This is definitely the last corner.”

Text block 2: “I have lied before.”

CTA: “How many ‘last corners’ is too many?”

### 18_group_chat

Text block 1: “The group chat said one hour.”

Text block 2: “Nobody believed that.”

CTA: “Tag the friend who says ‘quick ride.’”

### 19_wrong_route

Text block 1: “This is not the route.”

Text block 2: “It is, however, a better route.”

CTA: “Would you turn around?”

## One-minute narration outline

### 20_why_this_road

- Hook: “This road looks ordinary for about five seconds.”
- Setup: “Then the lane tightens, the trees close in, and every corner hides the next one.”
- Payoff: “That is why this route keeps winning.”
- Close: “Would you ride it, or choose the straight road?”

### 21_three_signs

- Hook: “Three things I look for before a blind bend.”
- Beat one: “First, the road surface.”
- Beat two: “Second, the sight line.”
- Beat three: “Third, what the warning signs are telling me.”
- Close: “Which one do you notice first?”

Keep safety language observational. Do not frame public-road speeding or risky maneuvering as a challenge.

## Audio mix target

- Narration clearly intelligible.
- Engine audio present but underneath the narration.
- No copyrighted music baked into the one-minute originals.
- If a native TikTok sound is used on a short clip, add it in TikTok after the narration timing is correct.
