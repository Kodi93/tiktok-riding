## Winner multiplication rule

At the 24-hour review:
- >=1.5x recent comparable median: immediately brief 2–3 adjacent concepts;
- strong likes/comments but weak views: keep premise, rewrite frame-zero hook;
- views without shares/profile activity: do not clone aggressively;
- three materially weak executions from one series: pause and rework.
