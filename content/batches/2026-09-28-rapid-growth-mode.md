## Winner multiplication

When a post beats 1.5x the recent comparable median, create three adjacent posts immediately:
- same emotional premise, new situation;
- same series, new punchline;
- same hook structure, new visual.

Do not repost the exact clip or rewrite only one line.

## Scheduling signal

Current Metricool audience peaks are strongest around:
- 10 AM ET
- noon ET
- 6 PM ET

Wednesday–Friday currently show the largest audience values. Treat this as an account-specific scheduling signal, not a universal TikTok rule.

## Metricool constraint

A scheduled 10 AM post on 2026-09-28 returned "You have reached your Metricool account limit." Do not pay to remove this constraint. Use TikTok-native posting/scheduling for overflow rather than adding software budget.
